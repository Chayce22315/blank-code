more than one brain

a whole dev team.
on your project.

blank code's lead agent plans the job and calls in the specialists it needs. small task? small team. giant game? scale the crew to the work.

agents should share relevant project knowledge, split independent tasks, and coordinate before changing the same systems. the lead verifies the result instead of blindly trusting a green checkmark.

you stay in control: inspect work, pause agents, review diffs, and roll back risky changes.
